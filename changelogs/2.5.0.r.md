# 2.5.0.r (Upcoming)
- ### New Features
	- The biome selector for each hex has been ***completely*** overhauled. @Barza
		- A word of warning: You will need to explore outwards to see the new system in action. You might also encounter some harshly mixed hexes at the divide between old terrain and new terrain. Sorry!
		- Hexes are no longer grouped up into regions and are instead divided up into three pools. Land, Coastal and Ocean.
		- Each pool pulls from a weighted list at random, meaning that biomes will be evenly distributed. New nether, and and void hexes will no longer be clumped together.
		- The system respects world seeds, the same seed will have the same biomes at the same hexes.
		- The list of available biomes has been greatly expanded. You will see some biomes that weren't generating before, and even some brand new ones!
		- The new distribution allows certain hex types, like void hexes, to be more common.
		- Some brand new biomes have been designed.
			- Three new end biomes themed around the content of Echovoids (@Micomi): 
				- Pearlescent Cliffs
				- Entropic Delta
				- Blonde Grove
			- Sculklands (@Barza): A land completely infested with sculk. You may go in to retrieve the valuable catalysts...but watch out!
			- Glimmering Weald (@Barza): Based on the one from Quark, this land is full of glowing fungi.
			- Mystical Grove (@Barza): An absolute treasure trove for the aspiring botanist. The land is infused with mana, turning the grass blue and causing mystical plants to sprout up everywhere. Some have even grown to be several blocks tall! All these flowers have attracted a large population of bees.
	- New recipe chain for IE's Graphite Electrodes, making the Arc Furnace usable @Nyagi
	- Several items and blocks in the newly added Echovoids were integrated into the current recipe chains @Nyagi
		- Note: said items are not required for current progression in order to avoid forcing far exploration.
	- The pure metal items now have a chemlib-style abbreviation, making them easier to tell apart. @Barza
		- The abbreviation is only visible in inventories
		- Float-separated dusts are marked with a *, to tell them apart from the pure dusts and indicate that they are not quite pure yet.
