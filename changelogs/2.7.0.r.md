# Compression Changelogs
Formatting:  
**2.[n].[m]** - Where **n** is the update within the tier being worked on and **m** is a minor update or hotfix  
**.b** - Beta release  
**.r** - Completed release version  
**.r.[n]** - Hotfix to a release version where **n** increments.

# 2.7.0.r

- ### Features
	- A new science has been added, Crude Geology (Nyagi)
		- The test is made from three components, each in their container:
			- A set of fitted picks, One for each Mohs Hardness from 1 to 10. Later on, a material of Mohs Hardness 11 can be added to improve the yield.
			- A sampling of Rare-Earth Sludges. The rare earth minerals come from an excavator vein.
			- An Undercrust Sampling, composed of deep samples and crude oil
		- To test this assortment of material, a Magimorphic Catalytic System is used.
			- It's a marimorphosis, mulched and turned into a catalyst to process items with. Yes, it means automating them.
	- The start of a new line of weapons is obtainable. Guns!
		- The first guns are obtained in late T1 with Cordite
		- Later tiers will have better sets of weaponry
