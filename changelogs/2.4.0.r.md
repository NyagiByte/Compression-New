Formatting:

# 2.4.0.r (Upcoming)
- ### New Features
	- The generation of boulders has been overhauled @ExXec and Micomi
		- Boulders generate at or near the center point of hexes
		- Boulders now use a custom selection system, which ensures a reasonably uniform distribution of boulder encounters.
			- In other words, you should be able to find one of each boulder before finding repeats.
		- Boulders no longer generate in nether hexes to give more space to fortresses and to prevent them being eaten up by basalt deltas.
		- The terrain around boulders should be significantly smoother and natural looking, instead of square slabs of ground.

	- The AE2 Growth Accelerator has been enabled to work on a lot more than just budding crystals and crops. @Barza
		- Technical note: Accelerators work with things that need random ticks to work. What follows is a list of the enabled things that use said random ticks.
		- Recrystallyzation stsps for 2,5-Dimethoxybenzoic Acid and 2,5-Dimethoxy-1,4-Benzenedicarboxylic Agic
		- Converting Organic Compost into Rich Soil
		- The actual Rich Soil. Have fun. (Also supplementaries planters)
		- Pointed Dripstone. Yes. Really.
		- Immersive Engineering Slag Gravel (It passes on growth ticks to kelp planted on it, boosting its growth speed.)
